Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my order? +
You can check your order status from the Orders section. The delivery status will be updated as your order moves towards you.
How can I cancel my order? +
Open your current order and select the cancellation option if it is available for your order.
How do I get a refund? +
If your order is eligible for a refund, the refund will be processed according to the applicable payment and refund policy.
My payment failed. What should I do? +
Please check your payment method and try again. If the amount was deducted, check your transaction status before attempting another payment.
I received the wrong item. +
Go to your order details and contact support with the details of the issue. Our support team can help you resolve the problem.

Still need help?

Our support team is here to help you with your questions and concerns.
